# Product and Scope Definition

## 1. Product definition

GROW is a long term education access, self discovery, capability discovery, learning, career exploration and student success platform. Its primary purpose is to help a person move from uncertainty about their direction toward a better informed next decision.

The product combines structured questions, open conversation, practical tasks, evidence collection, contextual career intelligence, human review and follow up. The long term participant experience is intended to feel like a thoughtful career consultant, initially accessible through WhatsApp and primarily in Pakistani Urdu.

## 2. Problem

Many students are asked to choose subjects, streams, degrees or careers before they have enough evidence about their own interests, abilities, values and constraints. Graduates and working adults can face the same problem after years of education or employment.

A one time quiz or a direct question about a desired career is insufficient for participants who genuinely do not know what they want. GROW must discover patterns through examples, behaviour, preferences, tasks and follow up rather than forcing an early answer.

## 3. Product outcome

A participant should progressively understand:

- Their current interest signals.
- Their demonstrated strengths and areas still developing.
- Their learning preferences and work values.
- The constraints that affect feasible pathways.
- Which education and career directions deserve exploration.
- Why a direction is being considered.
- What evidence supports or conflicts with it.
- What remains unknown.
- What low cost practical action to try next.

GROW does not promise perfect prediction or guaranteed employment, income or educational success.

## 4. Audience

The audience is defined by education or decision stage, not rigid age bands.

| Track | Typical stage | Primary job |
|---|---|---|
| School discovery | Grade 8 to Grade 10 | Explore interests and capabilities before subject or stream decisions |
| College direction | Intermediate, A Levels, diploma or equivalent | Compare degree, skill and training pathways |
| University and graduate | Undergraduate or recently graduated | Explore roles, skill gaps and entry pathways |
| Career reset | Working adult or returning worker | Explore transferable strengths and lower risk transition options |

The initial geography is Karachi, followed by Sindh and Punjab, then broader Pakistan. Expansion requires local education, language, labour market, family, access and safety context. It is not merely a marketing change.

## 5. Language and access

- Primary participant language: accessible Pakistani Urdu.
- English may be offered as an additional language.
- Future language candidates include Sindhi, Punjabi, Saraiki and Pashto.
- Smartphone access is the primary initial device assumption.
- The product must not equate weak English, limited device access, school type, gender, city or family income with low capability.
- Voice input is a future channel capability and is not currently implemented.

## 6. Product method

1. Context: collect only information needed to interpret later evidence.
2. Open discovery: invite stories, examples, preferences and reflection.
3. Structured assessment: explore multiple dimensions with versioned questions.
4. Evidence capture: preserve source, method, context, confidence and time.
5. Contradiction handling: retain opposing signals and require reviewed resolution.
6. Career intelligence: maintain versioned career, skill, pathway and market reference data.
7. Practical experiments: let participants try representative low cost tasks.
8. Recommendation: explain multiple plausible directions, evidence, tradeoffs and uncertainty.
9. Human review: review sensitive, conflicting or high consequence cases.
10. Follow up: learn whether participants understood and acted on guidance.

The first five elements have code backed foundations. Career reference data is implemented as a registry. Experiments, recommendations and participant delivery remain future work.

## 7. Operating and business assumptions

The source documentation describes an initial Karachi pilot, Urdu first, WhatsApp first operating model, standard fee PKR 500, reduced fee PKR 200, and free access after human review. These are documented operating assumptions, not verified current commercial policy. The owner must confirm them before payment or access logic is implemented.

The product should initially learn from a small, consented cohort rather than optimise for participant volume. Human review capacity and outcome quality must be measured before scaling acquisition or automation.

## 8. What GROW is not

- Not a generic AI chatbot.
- Not a one time personality quiz.
- Not a deterministic career prediction system.
- Not a course marketplace or affiliate engine.
- Not a motivational content product.
- Not a substitute for qualified mental health care.
- Not an automated poverty classifier.
- Not an autonomous authority for irreversible decisions about minors.
- Not a full institutional school management system in the initial release.

## 9. Scope boundaries

### Current code backed foundation

- Participant and identifier domain records.
- Profiles, consent records, guardian relationship records and referral sources.
- Participant journey and guarded state transitions.
- Versioned assessment definitions, sessions, responses, observations and evidence.
- Sufficiency and contradiction states.
- Human review and append only override records.
- Access decision records without a production fee policy.
- Follow up records.
- Audit events.
- Versioned career taxonomy, profiles, skills, pathways, research sources and market observations.
- Synthetic loaders and tests.

### Planned, not implemented

- Transparent participant career recommendation engine.
- Practical experiment assignment and submission workflow.
- Participant facing web application.
- Public marketing website.
- Production operator console.
- Production authentication, roles and permissions.
- Official WhatsApp Business Platform integration.
- AI provider adapter and AI assisted interpretation.
- Speech transcription and voice interaction.
- Payment processing.
- Production deployment, monitoring and operations.
- Mobile application and offline learning.

### Later product directions

- Interactive learning system.
- Expanded administrator dashboard.
- Mobile application and full web application.
- WhatsApp teacher experience.
- Offline learning.
- Voice conversations.
- Educational games.
- Institutional and partner products.

These later directions must be planned as separate phases. They must not be represented as existing capabilities.

## 10. Success principles

Early success should be judged by whether participants understand the process, complete meaningful discovery, receive specific and explainable guidance, can describe why suggested directions deserve attention, complete useful experiments, and take an informed next step. Completion and satisfaction alone are insufficient if the guidance is generic or unsupported.
